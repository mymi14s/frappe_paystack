# Version 15.5.0 Release Notes

## Highlights

- **Saved Cards**: store a customer's card after a successful charge and bill it again later, without asking them to check out a second time.
- **Subscription Auto-Collection**: invoices raised by ERPNext Subscriptions are charged to the customer's saved card on a daily pass, opt-in per company.
- **Refunds**: raise full or partial refunds from a payment, or have one raised automatically when a Credit Note is issued, with the reversal booked to the ledger and a receipt emailed.
- **Settlements**: import Paystack payouts and book the gross, fees and net to your bank, fee and suspense accounts, so the money that lands matches the money recorded.
- **Reconciliation**: a daily pass verifies every recent payment against Paystack and flags amount, currency and status mismatches, with a noted manual override for the ones you have already checked.
- **Point of Sale**: send a payment link to the shopper by email or phone from the POS, and watch the till for the balance as it is paid.
- **Webshop Checkout**: shoppers pay for a storefront order with Paystack, and a paid cart order is invoiced and billed on its own, with no one in the back office touching it.
- **Customer Portal**: customers see their payments and refunds at `/my-payments` and download PDF receipts.

### Other Features & Enhancements

- Invoices can carry the checkout link as a QR code, and payments and refunds have receipt print formats of their own.
- Three new reports: Paystack Activity, Paystack Settlements vs Ledger, and Paystack Unsettled Payments.
- Hardened webhooks: signature verification, an optional IP allowlist, rate limiting, and idempotent handling so a redelivered event never bills twice.
- A payment Paystack took but the ledger has not recorded is retried until it is booked, and anything still outstanding is listed on the workspace.
- Choose Paystack's inline popup or a hosted checkout page per gateway, and set how long a payment link stays open.
- A paid Sales Order is invoiced and a paid invoice is settled on its own, with the Payment Entry booked against it.
- An order with a payment in progress reads Processing Payment and cannot be paid a second time.
- Reports and dashboard cards respect company permissions, so a user restricted to one company sees only that company's money.
- Dunning documents can be collected through Paystack alongside Sales Invoices, Sales Orders and POS Invoices.

### New Doctypes

- **Paystack Customer Authorization**: the stored card a customer can be billed on again.
- **Paystack Refund Log**: a refund and the reversal entry it booked.
- **Paystack Settlement**: a Paystack payout and the journal entry that recorded it.
- **Paystack Reconciliation Log**: what Paystack says about a payment against what the ledger holds.
